mail-hub — Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Who uses this app

mail-hub is a personal tool used only by its developer, with the developer's own Google accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the account owner's consent, mail-hub accesses Gmail messages and Google Drive files of the signed-in account in order to search, read, organize and send email and to manage files, at the owner's request.

Where data is stored

The app runs on the owner's own computer. Access tokens are stored locally on that computer. mail-hub has no server of its own and does not copy Google user data to any server operated by the developer.

Sharing

The owner operates mail-hub through an AI assistant (Claude by Anthropic) under the owner's own account. When the owner asks the assistant to read a message or file, that content is processed by the assistant to complete the owner's request.

Google user data is not sold, not shared with any other third parties and not used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as the owner needs it for the task at hand. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; local tokens are deleted by removing them from the owner's computer.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/Superodmo/mail-hub.
